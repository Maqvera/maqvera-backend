FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How far in advance should I book?

We recommend booking at least 6–8 weeks ahead for Umrah and several months ahead for Hajj, to secure the best hotel and flight availability.

What's included in a package?

Every package clearly lists what's included — typically accommodation, transport, and visa processing. Flights and Ziyarat tours can be added.

Can I customize my package?

Yes — every package can be tailored to your group size, hotel preference, and budget. Contact us and we'll build a custom quote.

What support do I get during travel?

Our team is reachable 24/7 during your trip for any assistance you need, from the airport to your return home.
